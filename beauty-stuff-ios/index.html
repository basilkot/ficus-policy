Beauty Stuff for iPhone — Privacy Policy

In effect since 25 August 2026 · Русская версия

In short:

  • everything you put into the app — products, dates, photos, shopping list — is kept on your device only;
  • what leaves the device is crash reports and anonymous usage statistics, handled by the Google services described below;
  • a backup — and a picture of your year summary or your collage — leaves the app only when you ask for it, into the file or the app you choose;
  • no account is needed, there are no ads, the advertising identifier is not collected, and the data is not sold to anyone.

Who is responsible for the data

Beauty Stuff is developed and maintained by a private individual. Questions about data, deletion requests and anything else go to basilkot@gmail.com.

What is kept on the device

Everything the app holds stays in the phone memory and is never sent anywhere:

  • products — name, brand, category, note, price, the dates of purchase, opening and expiration, and the reason a product was archived;
  • product photos;
  • the lists of brands and categories;
  • the shopping list;
  • app settings — whether reminders are on, and at what time, for example.

The records live in the app database, the photos in its own directory. Neither is available to other apps. The home screen widget reads the same database and never leaves the device. Uninstalling Beauty Stuff erases all of it along with the app.

What leaves the device

The app uses two services from Google LLC. Both receive data automatically; nothing is required from you.

Crash reports — Firebase Crashlytics

When the app crashes or trips over an error, a technical report is sent:

  • the error type and the call stack;
  • device model, iOS version, amount of free memory;
  • app version and a technical installation identifier.

The contents of your collection, the photos and the product names never end up in a report.

Usage statistics — Google Analytics for Firebase

Anonymous information on how the app is used is collected: launches and session length, the screens opened, and a handful of events — a product added, a product archived, a backup saved or restored, reminders switched on. Product names, brand names and photos never end up in the statistics.

The app does not collect the device advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other apps and websites. That is why the system tracking prompt is never shown.

How Google handles the data it receives is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in the Firebase terms.

Backups

The backup you make

On “Save a backup” the app builds a single file with the products, the lists, the shopping list and the photos, and hands it to the system share dialog. Where the file goes — to Files, to iCloud Drive, to an email to yourself — is your choice. The app never sends the backup anywhere by itself and has no access to your other files.

The backup iOS makes

If iCloud Backup is enabled on the device, or you back the device up to a computer, the operating system saves the app data along with the product photos on its own. This is done by iOS, not by the app: Beauty Stuff has no access to the contents of that backup. It can be turned off in the device settings, under “iCloud → iCloud Backup”.

Pictures you share

The year summary and the collage become a picture only when you tap “Share”, and that picture goes to the same system dialog the backup uses: where it lands — a messenger, a note, the photo library — is your choice. The app sends it nowhere by itself.

The collage can show the products with the background removed. That is done on the device by the system framework, without a network and without sending the photos anywhere, and the original photos are not modified: the cut-out lives only while the screen is open.

Permissions and what they are for

  • Notifications — to remind you that a product is about to expire. The permission is asked only when you switch reminders on yourself in the app settings. If you decline, the app keeps working, but no reminders will arrive.
  • Camera — to take a photo of a product for its page. Asked the first time you take one. Without it you can still pick a photo you already have.

The app never asks for access to your photo library: a photo is picked in a system dialog that hands over the selected image and nothing else. Beauty Stuff has no access to any other photo on the device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

  • The data on the device is yours to control: any record can be edited or deleted in the app, and deleting the app erases everything at once.
  • You can take your data with you at any moment — “Save a backup” in the settings.
  • To ask for the crash reports and statistics tied to your installation to be deleted, write to basilkot@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the set of collected data changes, this text will be updated along with the app, and the date at the top of the document will change with it. By continuing to use the app after an update you accept the new version.
