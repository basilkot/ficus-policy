Subscription

What is included

Two tiers. The first unlocks:

  • reading the expiry date and the “12M” symbol from a photo of the packaging;
  • care routines and warnings about combining active ingredients;
  • collage templates;
  • syncing between devices and platforms.

The second includes all of the above and, on top of that, the AI features:

  • creating a product from a photo of its packaging;
  • reading the ingredient list from a photo of the label;
  • advice about your bag.

The second tier’s features are limited by a quota: 50 requests per calendar month, of which no more than 8 may be advice. Unused requests do not carry over to the next month, and extra ones cannot be bought separately. The remaining quota is visible in the app and on the purchase screen before payment. A request that failed through our fault does not spend quota; repeating the same request is free.

Everything not listed as paid stays free — including the bag itself, photos, reminders, the shopping list, statistics, backups and ingredients typed in by hand.

The AI features

Creating a product from a photo, reading ingredients and advice all work through a language model. Several things follow from that, and they are worth knowing before you buy.

  • The model makes mistakes. An ingredient list may be read incompletely or wrongly, especially from a blurred photo, small print or worn packaging. Check what was read against the packaging itself.
  • This is not medical or cosmetology advice. The alcohol, fragrance and allergen marks, the warnings about combinations and the advice about your bag are a reference hint. In case of allergies, pregnancy, a skin condition or medication, the decision belongs to a doctor, not to an app.
  • Send only your own photos — of packaging and labels. Photos of other people, of documents or of someone else’s material should not be sent.
  • Before the first request the app asks for your agreement, and these features can be switched off entirely in the settings. What exactly goes to the model and what is kept is described in the privacy policy.
