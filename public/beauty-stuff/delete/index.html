Beauty Stuff for Android — deleting your account and data

Updated 6 September 2026 · Русская версия

This page is about Makeup Bag — Beauty Stuff for Android (ru.basilkot.beautystuff), published by FicusApps. It explains how to delete the account the app creates when you sign in, and what happens to your data.

The app works without an account at all. Signing in is only needed for syncing and for the paid features; if you never signed in, there is nothing on our side to delete.

In the app — immediately

  1. Open Beauty Stuff and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The cloud copy and the account itself are gone at once. Your bag on this phone stays where it is — deleting the account does not touch it.

If you no longer have the app

Write to the address below from the email of the Google account you signed in with — that is how the request is matched to the data. Ask for the account and its data to be deleted. It is done by hand, and no later than 30 days from the request.

Deletion requests and questions

kot.julic@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • the cloud copy of your bag: products with all their fields, including notes and ingredients; the lists of brands and categories; routines; the shopping list; and the marks about what you deleted;
  • the photos of your products stored in the cloud, full-size ones and previews;
  • the account record itself: the email address of your Google account and the internal identifier your data was stored under.

What stays, and why

  • The bag on your phone. It was never in the cloud to begin with, and deleting the account leaves it alone. To remove it, uninstall the app or clear its data in the Android settings;
  • Your subscription and payment records at Google Play. They belong to Google, not to us — we only see whether a subscription is active. Cancel a subscription in Google Play; deleting the account here does not cancel it;
  • Crash reports and usage statistics. They are not linked to your account: they are tied to an installation identifier. To have them removed as well, write to the same address — the advertising identifier of the device is needed for that, and Android shows it in its own settings.

If you do nothing

The cloud copy lives for as long as you use it. After six months with no syncing it is deleted along with the photos and the account. A month before that we write to the email of your Google account, so that you have time to come back and lose nothing — opening the app is enough. We never delete without warning.
