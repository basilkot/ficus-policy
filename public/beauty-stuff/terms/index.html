Subscription

What is included

There are two tiers. The first one unlocks:

  • reading the expiry date and the “12M” symbol from a photo of the packaging;
  • care routines and warnings about clashing active ingredients;
  • collage templates;
  • syncing between devices.

The second includes all of the above and, on top of that, the AI features:

  • creating a product from a photo of the packaging;
  • reading the ingredient list from a photo of the label;
  • advice about your collection.

The second tier's features are limited by a quota: 50 requests per calendar month, of which no more than 8 may be advice. Unused requests do not carry over to the next month, and extra requests cannot be bought separately. What is left is visible in the app and on the purchase screen before payment. A request that failed through our fault does not spend quota; repeating the same request is free.

Everything not listed as paid stays free — including the collection itself, photos, reminders, the shopping list, backups, and ingredient lists typed in by hand.

The AI features

Creating a product from a photo, reading ingredients and advice all work through a language model. Several things follow from that, and they are worth knowing before you buy.

  • The model makes mistakes. An ingredient list may be read incompletely or wrongly, especially from a blurred shot, small print or worn packaging. Check what was read against the packaging itself.
  • This is not medical or cosmetological advice. The alcohol, fragrance and allergen marks, the warnings about clashing ingredients and the advice about your collection are a reference hint. With an allergy, during pregnancy, with a skin condition or while taking medication, the decision belongs to a doctor, not to an app.
  • Send only your own photos — of packaging and labels. Photos of other people, of documents, or of other people's material should not be sent.
  • What exactly travels to the model, and what of it is kept, is described in the privacy policy.
